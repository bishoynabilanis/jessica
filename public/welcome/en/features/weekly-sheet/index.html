🧾 Weekly sheet

A whole week on one page

No long report: one picture of what was really studied this week, and what still needs a second look.

Try it now ← All features

The problem

The week passes and nobody remembers what was studied or what is left.

How Jessica solves it

The sheet is built from the last seven days only, from data already on your device: minutes, finished topics, practice tests with their average, and mistakes ready to review. With no finished topic yet, it says a new week starts today.

No account and no server. The sheet is drawn on your device and leaves it only if you press Share, and you choose who gets it. The child's name is optional.

  • Study minutes and finished topics.
  • Practice tests and their average.
  • Mistakes ready to review next week.
  • Saved as an image and shared only by choice.

Step by step

1. Open the sheet

From Report, choose the Weekly study sheet.

2. Look it over

See the minutes, topics and mistakes, and add a name if you want.

3. Share or save

The sheet goes nowhere unless you press Share.

Who is it for?

For parents who want a quick picture of the week, and students planning the next one.

Questions

Is the sheet stored on your side?

No. It is made and kept on your device.

How is it different from the monthly report?

The sheet is one week on one page. The monthly report is a longer page for the whole month.
