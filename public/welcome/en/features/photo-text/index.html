📷 Text from a photo

Photograph the question and read the words

Photograph a question and the Read the text only button pulls the words out of the picture, with no key and no account.

Try it now ← All features

The problem

Not every question is typed text. A picture alone has no words to copy.

How Jessica solves it

Choose a photo of the question and press Read the text only (no AI key). Reading happens on your device and the photo stays on it.

The reading tool downloads once from the internet, so the first time needs a connection. If the photo is not clear, it says no text was found and you can try a sharper one.

  • Reads text with no key.
  • The photo never leaves your phone.
  • The tool downloads once from the internet.
  • A sharper photo reads better.

Step by step

1. Photograph the question

A clear, straight photo.

2. Read the text only

Press the button and wait.

3. If no text is found

Try a sharper photo.

Who is it for?

For students who photograph questions, and parents who do not want to enter a key.

Questions

Do I need internet?

Only the first time, to download the reading tool.

Is the photo uploaded?

No, it stays on your device.
