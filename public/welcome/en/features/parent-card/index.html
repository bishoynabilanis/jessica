🏅 Achievement card

Let parents see a win without a report

Instead of "did you study?", the student sends a card with a real achievement: a streak, a finished topic, a new level or this week's minutes.

Try it now ← All features

The problem

A student studies, but the parent does not see it, and nobody reads long reports.

How Jessica solves it

Jessica works out wins only from your real progress: a streak of 3 days or more, a finished topic, level 2 or higher, or 30 minutes of study in a week. The student picks one and sees the card.

Send on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with a short text and the app link, with no phone number. Share sends the card image, and Save keeps it. The child's name on the card is optional and off at the start. Nothing is stored.

  • Wins are real, built from your own progress only.
  • Send on WhatsApp, share the picture, or save it.
  • The name on the card is optional and off by default.
  • No storage and no account.

Step by step

1. Open Share a win

From the home screen or the Report page. It appears once you have a win.

2. Pick a win

Choose one you really earned, and add the name if you want.

3. Send

WhatsApp with text and a link, or share the card image, or save it.

Who is it for?

For students who want to make their family proud, and parents who want to see progress without pressure.

Questions

Does the card image go through the WhatsApp button?

The WhatsApp button sends text and the link only. The image goes through Share, or after you save it.

Does Jessica keep the card?

No. It is made on your device and we do not store it.
