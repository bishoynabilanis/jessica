🎒 Class pack

One link: the unit quiz and its cards

Instead of sending a paper and then cards, send one short pack that opens complete on the student's phone.

Try it now ← All features

The problem

One message with a quiz and another with cards, and the student loses one.

How Jessica solves it

The pack joins a unit quiz and study cards for the same unit into a compressed link or QR. Everything travels inside the link, so there is no server.

The cards are made with the AI key on the sender's device. With no key, or if it fails this time, the app says so and the pack is the quiz only. For the full pack, send the link and not the QR.

  • A unit quiz and its cards in one link.
  • No server, everything is inside the link.
  • With no key the pack is the quiz only.
  • For the full pack, send the link and not the QR.

Step by step

1. Pick the unit

In Class mode choose a unit from the book.

2. Make the pack

The app builds the quiz and the cards.

3. Send the link

The full link for the full pack.

Who is it for?

For a teacher preparing a whole class, and a parent who wants to send ready revision.

Questions

What if there is no AI key?

The pack is the unit quiz only, and the app tells you.

Why is the link better than the QR?

For the full pack the app itself advises sending the link and not the QR.
