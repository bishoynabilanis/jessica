🔬 Science board

Science in a few boxes instead of a long paragraph

Type, for example, "How does a plant make food?" and watch the idea move from box to box with arrows, instead of memorising a paragraph.

Try it now ← All features

The problem

Long science explanations are forgotten. The eye grasps order faster than a paragraph.

How Jessica solves it

Jessica sends your question to the AI provider you chose, with your own key, and draws the answer as a small board: numbered boxes and arrows.

The main warning: the board is made by AI from your question, not from your textbook, and it can be wrong. Check it against your book or ask your teacher. Your question goes only to your provider and nothing is uploaded to us.

  • Numbered boxes and arrows instead of a paragraph.
  • Needs your own AI key.
  • The question goes only to your provider.
  • It can be wrong, so check it against your book.

Step by step

1. Type a science question

Example: How does a plant make food?

2. Draw the board

Press Draw the board and wait a few seconds.

3. Check it with your book

Confirm the steps with the book or your teacher.

Who is it for?

For students who understand by picture, and parents explaining something simply.

Questions

Do I need a key?

Yes, your own AI key. A guide on the site shows how to get one.

Is the board always right?

No, it can be wrong. Always check it against your book.
