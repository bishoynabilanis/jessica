👁️ Comfortable reading

School books that are easier on the eyes

Small letters and crowded lines tire a student before the lesson even starts. Switch on Comfortable reading in Settings and the whole book changes.

Try it now ← All features

The problem

Many students give up on reading not because the lesson is hard, but because of how it looks: small letters, tight lines and a bright white page on a phone.

Parents rarely know how to fix this without special tools, so the student carries on tired.

How Jessica solves it

One switch in Comfort and accessibility makes the text bigger, widens the space between letters and lines, and swaps the background for a calm paper colour. A separate option switches to a font with clearer letter shapes.

This is a comfort setting, not a treatment and not a diagnosis. If you are worried about dyslexia, a specialist should assess it. Your choices are saved on your phone only.

  • Bigger text and wider spacing between letters and lines.
  • A calm paper colour instead of bright white.
  • An optional clearer font.
  • Saved on the device. No account and nothing is sent.

Step by step

1. Open Settings

From the menu choose Settings and scroll to Comfort and accessibility.

2. Switch on Comfortable reading

Turn it on, and pick the clearer font if you like.

3. Open your book

Go back to the book or lesson and see the difference. Change it any time.

Who is it for?

For students who tire during long reading, parents who notice a child losing their place between lines, and adults too.

Questions

Does this treat dyslexia?

No. It makes reading easier on the eyes, but it is not a treatment or a diagnosis.

Is it saved when I close the app?

Yes, on your device. You can change it in Settings.
