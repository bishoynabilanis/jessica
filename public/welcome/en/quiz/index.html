How to read the result

A score of 8 to 10 means the grade's core skills are in place and the next step is speed and harder word problems. Five to seven means the basics are there with a gap or two — usually one operation that has not settled. Under five means the grade is ahead of the child right now, and it is worth stepping back one grade and rebuilding, which is faster than pushing forward.

The test samples the core skills, it does not cover every lesson. When you open the app after it, the questions your child missed become cards in the mistakes notebook, and the bank keeps generating fresh papers on exactly those skills until they hold.
