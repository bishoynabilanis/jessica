A question bank that never runs out

📖 4 minute read · updated November 2026

A printed question book has a fixed number of questions, and once you have solved them it is done. Jessica's bank does the opposite: every drill is generated again on the device from your own grade, with a reason after every answer, no internet and no key.

Why it beats a printed question book

  • No fixed paper: every press of “start the drill” brings new numbers and words, so there is nothing to memorise and no answer sheet to copy.
  • A reason, not a key: after each answer the app says why — the law, the rule, or why the other option fails.
  • A real timed exam: twenty questions in twenty minutes, answers locked until you hand it in, and the clock marks the paper for you at zero.
  • Marking that names the weak spot: a score per skill, and the weakest one leads the result and the skills map.
  • A mistakes notebook: every miss becomes a card that comes back, not a red cross you forget.
  • Offline: the questions are generated on the device, so a drill opens on the bus with no account.

Example, Mathematics: “A rectangle is 8 cm by 5 cm. What is its perimeter?”
Options: 13 · 26 · 40 · 80
Why: perimeter = 2 × (length + width) = 2 × 13 = 26 cm.

Three steps

  1. Pick a subject: Mathematics, Science, English or Arabic.
  2. Pick ten questions for a quick drill, or twenty for the exam.
  3. Start. In practice mode the answer and the reason open immediately; in exam mode they wait until you hand in.

The skills follow the grade: counting and addition in primary, speed, pressure, density, force and Ohm's law in preparatory, and in English the plurals, tenses, comparatives and prepositions. The bank reads the grade from your settings, so it never asks a question above your level.

The timed exam

⏱️ 20 questions · 20 minutes · no help

The paper opens with a visible clock, you can move between questions and change any answer before handing in, and nothing is revealed mid-paper. If the time runs out, the paper is handed in for you.

After that: the score, a bar per skill, and the whole paper marked question by question — your answer, the right one and the reason.

Print it

Not every study session happens on a screen. One button turns the current drill into an A4 sheet: name, date and score at the top, the questions with four lettered options, then the answer key with the reason for each one. Every print is generated fresh, and the sheet saves as a PDF from the print dialog if you would rather send it than print it.

Unit review — from your unit, not the whole subject

The bank reads the last book you opened and the unit you are actually in, then builds twenty questions in a clear order: the quiz already saved for that unit, then questions written from its own pages (the terms it repeats and the sentences they came from), and only then the subject bank fills the rest. The result tells you how many questions came from the unit itself.

The skills map does the same at a finer grain: beside any skill there is a practise button that opens a ten-question paper on that skill alone — useful when you know the problem is “equations” and nothing else.

There is also a weekly paper: ten questions aimed at the four skills your own answers say are weakest, with a line comparing it to last week's paper.

Try a ten-question drill now — two minutes and you know where you stand.

Frequently asked

Do the questions repeat?

Every drill is generated again on your device with new numbers and words, so there is no fixed paper to memorise. Within one drill the same prompt never appears twice.

Do I need internet or an AI key?

No. The questions come from safe templates inside the app, so the bank works offline and needs no key. A key is only used by the features that read your own book pages.

What does the timed exam do?

Twenty questions in twenty minutes, answers hidden until you hand it in or the clock runs out. Then the whole paper is marked question by question with your answer, the right one and the reason.

Where do my mistakes go?

Every wrong answer becomes a card in the mistakes notebook, with its explanation. The bank can drill those mistakes later, and a question you get right leaves the notebook.

Can I print the paper?

Yes. One button turns the current drill into an A4 sheet with name, date and score lines, four lettered options, then the answer key with a reason for each answer.

Which subjects are covered?

Mathematics, Science, English and Arabic, with skills that follow the grade: arithmetic and geometry in primary, speed, pressure, density and Ohm's law in preparatory.
