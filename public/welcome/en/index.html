Install it on any device

Phone, tablet, iPad or computer — one tap and it behaves like a native app.

📱 iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Tap “Add”.

🤖 Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  4. Confirm.

💻 Computer

  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Click “Install”.
  4. It opens in its own window with a desktop shortcut.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — completely free and open source. You only need a free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.

Does it need internet?

The first load and book import need a connection. After that, reading, cards and saved exams work offline; AI features need internet.

Where is my data?

All on your device: the key in your browser, books in local storage. No server and no account.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open the link in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Does it work for government, private and language schools?

Yes. Pick your school type (government, private, language or Al-Azhar) and the subjects and books adapt — language schools also get French and German.

What if my subject is missing?

Import any PDF from your device and Jessica will index and teach from it.
